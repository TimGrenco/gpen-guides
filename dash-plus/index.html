How to use

  1. 1

    Charge

    • Plug any USB‑C cable into the charging port on the side.
    • Give it a full charge before first use — takes about 1 hour.

    USB‑C cable included.

  2. 2

    Load

    • Remove the magnetic mouthpiece.
    • Fully load the titanium chamber with ground dry herb.
    • Pack lightly with the included pick tool — do not overpack.
    • Re-attach the mouthpiece before powering on.

    ~0.3 g capacity. A proper pack allows airflow — overpacking restricts draws and reduces vapor quality.

  3. 3

    Activate

    • Hold the power button for 4 seconds to turn on. The display lights up.
    • Press Left / Right to adjust your target temperature.
    • Double-click to start a session. The device vibrates twice when it reaches temperature (~20 seconds).
    • The session auto-ends when the countdown hits 0:00. The device goes to standby.

  4. 4

    Draw

    • Once the device vibrates, wait 30 seconds for your material to bake, then draw slowly and steadily through the mouthpiece.
    • The spiral ceramic air path cools vapor before it reaches you.
    • The device powers off automatically after 1 minute of no activity.

    After your session: let the device cool, then remove the mouthpiece and brush out the chamber and mouthpiece filter screen. Regular cleaning keeps flavor and airflow at their best.

  5. 5

    Clean

    • Power it off and let it cool completely first.
    • Dip a cotton swab in isopropyl alcohol (ISO) and squeeze out the excess.
    • Swab around the silicone insert, over the screen, and above and inside the heating chamber.
    • For resin buildup, remove the filter screen and ceramic spiral air path and soak them in ISO.
    • Wipe them with a dry swab and let them air dry before putting them back in the mouthpiece.

    Never soak the device in alcohol or any liquid.

Help

Common questions
Device won't turn on

Hold the power button for a full 4 seconds. If nothing happens, charge the device — the battery may be depleted. The LED display lights up when it's on.

How do I know it's ready to use?

After you double-click to start a session, the device vibrates twice when it reaches your set temperature. The session countdown then begins on the display.

Vapor is thin or tastes off

Try a finer grind and make sure the chamber isn't overpacked. Clean the mouthpiece and air path regularly — residue buildup is the most common cause of reduced vapor and off flavor.

What do the error codes mean?

The Dash+ shows a code when something needs attention. Overheating: turn it off, wait five minutes, and turn it back on. E02 (temperature sensor problem) or E08 (heating coil not connected): contact G Pen support. If the overheating message keeps coming back, contact support too.

What does the warranty cover?

The Dash+ comes with a 2‑Year Limited Warranty covering electronics and manufacturing defects. Physical damage is not covered.
