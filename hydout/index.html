How to use

4 steps
  1. 1

    Charge it first

    • Plug any USB-C cable into the port on the base.
    • The display shows the battery level while it charges.
    • Give it a full charge before the first session.

    A USB-C cable isn't included — use the one you already carry.

  2. 2

    Load your cart

    • Pull off the magnetic mouthpiece cover.
    • Screw in your 510 cartridge — snug, not cranked.
    • Snap the cover back on. Your cart is now hidden and shielded from light.

    Fits most 510-thread cartridges up to 2g. Cartridge sold separately.

  3. 3

    Learn the button

    One button does everything. Tap a command to see what the display does.

    OFFDevice asleep

    Heat settings

    2.4V3.8V

    Lower heat brings out more flavor and stretches a cart further. Higher heat gives you bigger, warmer clouds.

    The Hydout shuts itself off after 2 minutes of no use. Five presses wakes it back up.

  4. 4

    Keep it clean

    • Use a cotton swab with isopropyl alcohol.
    • Wipe the inside of the mouthpiece cover.
    • Wipe the connection point where the cart meets the battery.
    • Let it dry fully before loading a cart again.

    Never soak the battery in alcohol or any liquid.

Common questions

Nothing happens when I press the button

The device auto-sleeps after 2 minutes. Press five times to wake it. If the display still doesn't light up, charge it with a USB-C cable and try again.

My cart isn't producing vapor

Make sure the cartridge is threaded in snugly — not over-tightened, which can push the connection pin down. Try a double-press for a 10-second preheat to loosen thick oil, then hold the button as you draw.

Which heat setting should I use?

Start low. Lower voltages give you more flavor and make a cart last longer; higher voltages give bigger, warmer clouds. Press three times to move through the five settings and find yours.

Will my cartridge fit?

The Hydout takes standard 510-thread cartridges up to 2g. Very wide or extra-tall carts may keep the magnetic cover from seating flush.

How do I know when it's charged?

The LED display shows the battery level. Leave it on the cable until it reads full.
