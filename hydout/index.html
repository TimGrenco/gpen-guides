Help

Common questions
Nothing happens when I press the button

The device auto-sleeps after 2 minutes. Press five times to wake it. If the display still doesn't light up, charge it and try again.

My cart isn't producing vapor

Make sure the cartridge is threaded in snugly — not over-tightened. Try a double-press for a 10-second preheat to loosen thick oil, then hold the button as you draw.

Which heat setting should I use?

Start low. Lower voltages give more flavor and make a cart last longer; higher voltages give bigger, warmer clouds. Press three times to cycle through the five settings.

Will my cartridge fit?

The Hydout takes standard 510-thread cartridges up to 2g. Very wide or extra-tall carts may keep the magnetic cover from seating flush.

How do I know when it's fully charged?

The LED display shows the battery level. Leave it on the cable until it reads full.

What does the warranty cover?

The Hydout carries a limited warranty. Register your device and read the full terms on gpen.com, or contact support with your order details.
