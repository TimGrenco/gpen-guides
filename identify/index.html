Think it's an Elite II?

The Elite II is taller and slimmer than the Dash II and Dash+, with a glossy black front and a silver mouthpiece on top. The Dash II has a round + and − button pad. The Dash+ has a square button above a color screen.

Dash II or Dash+?

The Dash+ has a color screen with a round temperature ring. The Dash II has a round + and − button pad and a small display near the bottom.

Hydout or Micro II?

Both have an angled top. Pull it off: if there's an oil cartridge inside, it's a Hydout. If there's a ceramic chamber for concentrates, it's a Micro II.

Micro II or Micro+?

The Micro+ is a slim pen with a tapered mouthpiece. The Micro II is wider, with an angled mouthpiece and a textured grip band below it.

Hyer or Connect?

Both attach to a glass water pipe. On the Hyer, the battery is a separate box joined to the heater by a cable. On the Connect, the battery clips straight onto the side of the tank.
