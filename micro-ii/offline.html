How to use

  1. 1

    Charge

    • Plug any USB‑C cable into the port on the device.
    • Give it a full charge before first use.

    USB‑C cable not included.

  2. 2

    Load

    • Remove the mouthpiece to access the ceramic heating chamber.
    • Load the chamber with your concentrate. Do not overfill.

    A small amount of concentrate goes a long way. Overfilling can clog the chamber and reduce airflow.

  3. 3

    Activate

    5×
    Turn on Five quick presses
    3×
    Adjust temperature 1 cloud = ~295°F (low) · 2 = ~340°F (medium) · 3 = ~395°F (high)
    2×
    Start session mode Double-tap to begin a 20‑second cycle
    2×
    Cancel session mode Double-tap again to stop early
    hold
    Manual heat Hold to heat while you draw, up to 25 seconds
    5×
    Turn off Auto shut-off after 10 minutes

    Lower temperatures preserve flavor terpenes. Higher temperatures produce bigger, more intense vapor.

  4. 4

    Clean

    • Allow the device to completely cool before cleaning.
    • Use a cotton swab + isopropyl alcohol (ISO).
    • Clean inside the ceramic heating chamber and the mouthpiece, and make sure the air holes are clear.
    • Allow the device to completely dry before using again.

    Never soak the battery in alcohol or any liquid.

  5. 5

    Alerts

    • G logo flashing = the tank isn't screwed in all the way, or the battery is dead. Screw the tank in firmly; if it keeps flashing, charge the device.

Help

Common questions
Nothing happens when I press the button

Press the button five times quickly to turn the device on. The LED will light up when it is powered on. If it still doesn't respond, charge the device first.

Which temperature should I use?

Start at ~295°F for the most flavorful, terpene-rich experience. Step up to ~340°F for a balance of flavor and vapor. Use ~395°F for maximum vapor production.

The G logo keeps flashing

A flashing G logo means the tank isn't screwed in all the way, or the battery is dead. Remove the mouthpiece, screw the tank in firmly, and try again. If it keeps flashing, charge the device. If it still flashes after charging, contact G Pen support.

How do I use the device with a glass rig?

Remove the mouthpiece from the Micro II and attach the Rig Adapter (sold separately). Connect 14 mm glass to your compatible glass rig for water-cooled vapor.

How often should I clean the chamber?

Clean the ceramic heating chamber after every few sessions to maintain performance. Let the device cool fully, then use a cotton swab with ISO to wipe the chamber and mouthpiece, and dry completely before use.

What does the warranty cover?

The Micro II comes with a 6‑Month Limited Warranty, or a 1‑Year Limited Warranty when you register your device at gpen.com. Read the full terms on gpen.com, or contact support with your order details.
