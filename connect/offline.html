Using your

Connect

  1. 1

    Charge

    • Plug a Micro USB cable into the port on the side of the battery housing.
    • Red LED = charging · Green LED = fully charged.
    • LED blinks 8 times when the battery is low.
  2. 2

    Set Up

    • Twist the tank onto the top of the adapter.
    • Twist the male glass adapter onto the bottom of the adapter.
    • Insert the adapter into the 14mm female joint of your water piece.

    14mm male adapter fits standard water pieces. Other adapter sizes available separately.

  3. 3

    Load

    • Remove the tank cover and load concentrate onto the ceramic plate inside.
    • Reattach the tank cover.
    • Attach the battery to the tank to complete the connection.
  4. 4

    Heat & Draw

    • Click 5 times to power on. LED lights up in your current voltage color.
    • Draw through your water piece while heating.

    After consecutive max-heat sessions: Wait at least 2 minutes before touching the tank cover.

Help

Common questions
Device won't turn on

Click the button 5 times quickly. Make sure the battery is attached to the tank — the connection must be complete for power to work. If nothing happens, charge the device first.

LED blinks 3 times on startup

Three blinks usually means a short circuit or the tank isn't properly connected. Remove the battery, check the tank connection, reattach firmly, and try again.

Vapor is thin or has poor flavor

Try a higher voltage setting (3× to cycle). Make sure the concentrate is on the ceramic plate, not the walls of the chamber. Clean the tank regularly — residue buildup is the most common cause of reduced performance.

Tank cover is too hot to handle

This is normal after consecutive max-heat sessions. Wait at least 2 minutes before touching the tank cover. The tank surface is designed to get hot — that's how it vaporizes concentrate efficiently.
