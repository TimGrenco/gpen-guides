How to use

  1. 1

    Charge

    • Plug any USB‑C cable into the port on the device.
    • Give it a full charge before first use.

    USB‑C cable not included.

  2. 2

    Load

    • Remove the magnetic mouthpiece.
    • Fully load the chamber with ground dry herb.
    • Pack lightly with the included pick tool. Do not overpack.
    • Re-attach the mouthpiece before powering on.

    ~0.4 g capacity. A proper pack allows airflow. Overpacking restricts draws and reduces vapor quality.

    Clean

    • Clean the mouthpiece filter screen & bowl after every use with the pick tool.

Help

Common questions
How do I turn it on?

Hold the main button for 3 seconds. The device will vibrate and the display will turn on, indicating it is powered up and ready.

How do I start a session?

Once the device is on, set your temperature and double-tap the button within 2 seconds to start a session. It vibrates three times when it's heated and ready. Double-tap again anytime to cancel it early.

My vapor tastes burnt. What's wrong?

You may have the temperature too high, or the bowl may need cleaning. Try a lower temperature setting and clean the screen and bowl with the pick tool after each session.

How tight should I pack the chamber?

Pack firmly enough that material won't fall out but loose enough that you can draw air through easily. Overpacking restricts airflow and reduces vapor production.

How do I deep clean the mouthpiece?

Remove the mouthpiece insert and soak or wipe it with isopropyl alcohol (ISO). Let it dry completely before reassembling and using the device.
