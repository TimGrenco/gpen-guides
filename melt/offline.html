Using your

Melt

Register your product →
  1. 1

    Charge

    • Plug any USB-C cable into the charging port on the bottom.
    • Give it a full charge before first use — takes about 1 hour.
    • The LED blinks 8 times when the battery is low.

    Always ready: Melt supports pass-through charging — you can use it while it's plugged in. USB-C cable not included.

  2. 2

    Power On & Scoop

    • Click 5 times quickly to power on. The LED lights to confirm.
    • Touch the tip to your concentrate to scoop up a small amount.
    • Device auto-shuts off after 10 minutes of inactivity.
    • Click 5 times again to power off.
    5×
    Power on / off Click button 5 times quickly
  3. 3

    Heat & Drop

    • Hold the button to heat the ceramic tip — it heats for up to 5 seconds per hold.
    • Let your concentrate melt cleanly into your rig, bowl, or device.
    • Works with bangers, e-rigs, and G Pen devices like the Micro II, Micro+, Connect, and Hyer.
    Hold
    Heat tip Hold to heat, release to stop — 150 °C / 302 °F
  4. 4

    Clean

    • While the tip is still warm, wipe it with an ISO-soaked cotton swab.
    • Do not soak or submerge the device.
    • Regular cleaning keeps the tip performing at its best.

Help

Common questions
Nothing happens when I press the button

Press the button five times quickly to power on. The LED will light up to confirm. If it still doesn't respond, charge the device and try again.

How do I use the Melt?

Power it on (5× click), touch the ceramic tip to your concentrate to scoop it up, hold the button to heat, and let it melt cleanly into your device.

Does the Melt have variable heat?

No — the Melt uses a precisely tuned heating element optimized for smooth, controlled melting without burning. No settings to adjust.

Can I use Melt while it's charging?

Yes. The Melt supports USB-C pass-through charging — plug it in and keep using it without waiting.

Is the Melt safe for travel?

Yes. Its compact size (3.94" tall) and the included protective travel cap make it easy to carry. The 5× click lock prevents accidental activation.

What does the warranty cover?

The Melt includes a 90-day limited warranty covering electronics. It does not cover physical damage. Register your device and contact support with your order details.
